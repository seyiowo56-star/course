Career Opportunity

Vacancy: Receptionist / Front Desk Officer

IBTECH Computer Training is a technology and digital training organization committed to equipping individuals with practical computer and digital skills for education, employment and entrepreneurship.

We are looking for a friendly, professional and well-organized Receptionist / Front Desk Officer to join our team and serve as the first point of contact for students, visitors, clients and other stakeholders.

Female candidates are particularly encouraged to apply.

Key Responsibilities

  • Welcome and attend to students, visitors, clients and guests professionally.
  • Answer phone calls, respond to enquiries and provide accurate information about IBTECH training programmes and services.
  • Register and assist students with enquiries, admissions and basic administrative processes.
  • Maintain a professional and welcoming reception area.
  • Manage incoming and outgoing correspondence and messages.
  • Schedule appointments and assist with daily administrative activities.
  • Keep basic records of visitors, students, enquiries and office activities.
  • Assist management and other staff with administrative tasks when required.
  • Support the promotion of IBTECH training programmes through social media.
  • Create and publish simple social media content, including posts, announcements, pictures and training updates.
  • Respond to basic enquiries and messages received through social media platforms.
  • Assist in taking photographs and videos and documenting IBTECH activities when required.
  • Perform other duties related to front-desk and office administration as assigned.

Requirements

  • Minimum of SSCE, OND, HND or equivalent qualification.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Friendly, confident and professional personality.
  • Good computer literacy and proficiency in Microsoft Office applications.
  • Familiarity with social media platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and LinkedIn.
  • Ability to create and assist with simple social media content.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Must be punctual, responsible, presentable and willing to learn.

Female candidates are particularly encouraged to apply.

Preferred Skills

  • Communicate confidently and professionally with students and visitors.
  • Be comfortable speaking with people in person and over the phone.
  • Use smartphones effectively for taking pictures, videos and posting content.
  • Have an interest in social media and digital content creation.
  • Have basic knowledge of Canva and similar design tools.
  • Be proactive and able to handle multiple tasks.
  • Have a positive attitude and strong customer-service orientation.

Working Schedule

  • Days: Monday – Thursday
  • Time: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

What We Offer

  • A professional and friendly working environment.
  • Opportunity to gain administrative, communication and digital skills.
  • Practical experience in the technology and training sectors.
  • Opportunity to develop social media and digital content skills.
