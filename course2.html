Certificate in Computer Fundamental

Duration: 1 Month Certificate Course

The Certificate in Computer Fundamental course is designed to introduce students to the basic concepts and practical use of computers. It provides learners with the essential knowledge and skills required to operate a computer confidently in different working environments.

This course is suitable for beginners who want to develop their computer literacy and understand how computers work. Students will learn basic computer operations, document management, internet usage and other important computer applications.

Through practical training and guided exercises, participants will gain the confidence needed to use computers effectively at home, school and in the workplace.

What You Will Learn

At the end of this course, students should be able to:

  • Understand the basic components of a computer.
  • Identify different types of computer hardware and software.
  • Operate a computer and manage files and folders.
  • Understand basic computer applications.
  • Browse the internet and use online resources.
  • Apply basic computer skills in everyday activities.

Course Content

1. Introduction to Computers

  • Meaning and history of computers
  • Types and generations of computers
  • Characteristics of computers
  • Uses and importance of computers
  • Computer hardware and software

2. Computer Components and Operations

  • Input and output devices
  • Central Processing Unit (CPU)
  • Memory and storage devices
  • Basic computer operations
  • Starting and shutting down a computer

3. Introduction to Operating Systems

  • Understanding operating systems
  • Introduction to Microsoft Windows
  • Desktop, icons and taskbar
  • Managing files and folders
  • Basic system settings

4. Introduction to the Internet

  • Meaning and uses of the internet
  • Introduction to web browsers
  • Searching for information online
  • Introduction to email
  • Internet safety and security

Practical Training

Our training approach focuses on hands-on learning. Students will have the opportunity to practise computer operations and complete practical tasks under the guidance of experienced instructors.

  • Hands-on computer operation
  • Typing and keyboard practice
  • File and folder management
  • Internet browsing exercises
  • Basic troubleshooting exercises

Benefits of the Course

  • Develop essential computer literacy skills.
  • Improve confidence in using computers.
  • Gain practical knowledge for everyday activities.
  • Prepare for more advanced computer courses.
  • Receive a certificate upon successful completion.

Certificate

Students who successfully complete the training requirements will receive a Certificate in Computer Fundamental from IBTECH Computer Training.
