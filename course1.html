Diploma in Digital Marketing

Home Courses Digital Marketing
Duration: 3 Months
Course Type: Diploma
Practical Training

Course Overview

The Diploma in Digital Marketing at IBTECH Computer Training is designed to equip students with the practical knowledge and skills needed to promote businesses, products and services through digital platforms.

Students will learn how to develop digital marketing strategies, create engaging content, manage social media platforms, run online advertisements, improve website visibility and analyse marketing performance.

The programme combines theoretical knowledge with practical exercises to prepare students for real-world digital marketing opportunities.

Course Objectives

By the end of this programme, students should be able to:

  • Understand the fundamentals of digital marketing.
  • Develop effective digital marketing strategies.
  • Create and manage social media business accounts.
  • Design engaging digital marketing content.
  • Understand search engine optimisation (SEO).
  • Run and manage online advertising campaigns.
  • Use digital tools to measure marketing performance.
  • Promote businesses and products online effectively.

Course Requirements

  • Basic computer knowledge is an advantage.
  • Basic understanding of internet usage.
  • Interest in business, advertising and online promotion.
  • Willingness to participate in practical activities.
  • No previous digital marketing experience is required.

Course Content

Module 1: Introduction to Digital Marketing

  • Meaning and importance of digital marketing.
  • Traditional marketing vs digital marketing.
  • Digital marketing channels and platforms.
  • Understanding online customers and target audiences.

Module 2: Social Media Marketing

  • Introduction to social media marketing.
  • Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn marketing.
  • Creating and managing business pages.
  • Building an online audience and engagement.
  • Social media content planning.

Module 3: Content Creation and Design

  • Introduction to digital content creation.
  • Creating marketing graphics using Canva.
  • Writing engaging captions and promotional messages.
  • Creating short promotional videos.
  • Developing a content calendar.

Module 4: Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)

  • Understanding search engines.
  • Introduction to keywords and keyword research.
  • On-page and off-page SEO.
  • Website optimisation basics.
  • Improving website visibility on search engines.

Module 5: Online Advertising

  • Introduction to paid digital advertising.
  • Facebook and Instagram advertising.
  • Google Ads fundamentals.
  • Creating and managing advertising campaigns.
  • Understanding advertising budgets and targeting.

Module 6: Email and WhatsApp Marketing

  • Introduction to email marketing.
  • Creating effective marketing emails.
  • Building customer mailing lists.
  • WhatsApp Business setup and management.
  • Customer communication and follow-up strategies.

Module 7: Analytics and Performance Measurement

  • Introduction to digital marketing analytics.
  • Understanding reach, impressions and engagement.
  • Monitoring social media performance.
  • Introduction to Google Analytics.
  • Measuring and improving campaign results.

Module 8: Practical Project and Assessment

  • Developing a digital marketing strategy.
  • Creating a business social media campaign.
  • Designing promotional content.
  • Preparing a digital marketing portfolio.
  • Final practical assessment and presentation.

Additional Skills You Will Gain

  • Business branding and online presence development.
  • Basic copywriting and promotional communication.
  • Customer engagement and online reputation management.
  • Digital marketing planning and reporting.
  • Practical use of modern digital marketing tools.

Career Opportunities

Graduates of this programme can explore opportunities in different areas of digital marketing, including:

  • Digital Marketing Assistant
  • Social Media Manager
  • Content Creator
  • SEO Assistant
  • Online Advertising Assistant
  • Freelance Digital Marketer
  • Small Business Digital Marketing Consultant

Certification

Students who successfully complete the programme and meet the required assessment standards will receive a Diploma in Digital Marketing from IBTECH Computer Training.
