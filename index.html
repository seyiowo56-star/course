About IBTECH

Welcome to IBTECH Computer Training

IBTECH COMPUTER TRAINING LTD was founded in 2021 by a group of enthusiastic, close-knit, young, ambitious and skilled professionals to provide an avenue for people interested in building a career in information technology using the latest technological developments and innovations.

IBTECH Computer Training offers short, intensive and personalized courses that present new concepts and techniques through interactive classroom training, practical sessions and the application of theory. We prepare people to use technology to enhance their productivity in the workplace.

The institution is based in Zaria, Kaduna State, Nigeria. Our focus is to enable knowledge transfer and practical learning in computer courses such as computer basics, web design and development, software development and Microsoft Office packages.

We also provide consultancy, sales and supply of ICT infrastructure, installation and maintenance, among other services. We provide a conducive and ethical environment for teaching and learning professional computing and information technology courses.

We are committed to providing outstanding and affordable services and working with our customers to understand their career focus and explore valuable opportunities that suit their needs.

Thank you for visiting IBTECH Computer Training.
