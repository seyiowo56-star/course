Diploma in Microsoft Office Suite

Duration: 3 Months Diploma Course

The Diploma in Microsoft Office Suite is designed to equip students with essential office productivity skills using Microsoft Office applications. The course provides practical knowledge of creating professional documents, performing calculations, organizing information and preparing presentations.

Students will receive comprehensive training in Microsoft Word, Microsoft Excel, Microsoft PowerPoint, Microsoft Access and other useful office tools. The programme combines theoretical lessons with hands-on practical exercises to ensure that students develop the skills required in modern workplaces.

Whether you are a student, business owner, job seeker or working professional, this course will help you improve your productivity and become more confident in using Microsoft Office applications.

What You Will Learn

At the end of this course, students should be able to:

  • Create, format and edit professional documents using Microsoft Word.
  • Perform calculations and organize data using Microsoft Excel.
  • Create attractive and professional presentations using Microsoft PowerPoint.
  • Understand database creation and management using Microsoft Access.
  • Manage office information and digital documents.
  • Apply Microsoft Office skills to real-life business and workplace tasks.

Course Content

1. Microsoft Word

Learn how to create, edit, format and manage professional documents using Microsoft Word.

  • Introduction to Microsoft Word
  • Understanding the Word interface
  • Creating, saving and opening documents
  • Text formatting and paragraph alignment
  • Working with fonts, colours and styles
  • Creating and formatting tables
  • Inserting pictures, shapes and symbols
  • Page layout, margins and orientation
  • Headers, footers and page numbering
  • Creating letters, reports and professional documents
  • Mail Merge and document printing

2. Microsoft Excel

Develop spreadsheet skills for calculations, data organization, analysis and reporting.

  • Introduction to Microsoft Excel
  • Understanding workbooks, worksheets and cells
  • Entering and editing data
  • Formatting rows, columns and cells
  • Basic mathematical operations
  • Working with formulas and functions
  • SUM, AVERAGE, MIN and MAX functions
  • IF and COUNT functions
  • Sorting and filtering data
  • Creating charts and graphs
  • Preparing reports and spreadsheets
  • Page setup and spreadsheet printing

3. Microsoft PowerPoint

Learn how to design attractive presentations for academic, business and professional purposes.

  • Introduction to Microsoft PowerPoint
  • Creating and saving presentations
  • Working with slides and layouts
  • Adding and formatting text
  • Inserting pictures, shapes and icons
  • Using themes and slide designs
  • Applying transitions and animations
  • Inserting tables, charts and SmartArt
  • Presentation delivery techniques
  • Printing and exporting presentations

4. Microsoft Access

Understand the basics of database management and how to organize information efficiently.

  • Introduction to database management
  • Understanding Microsoft Access interface
  • Creating and saving databases
  • Creating and managing tables
  • Understanding fields and records
  • Setting primary keys
  • Creating relationships between tables
  • Working with queries
  • Creating forms for data entry
  • Generating reports

5. Microsoft Outlook and Office Productivity

Learn useful office communication and productivity skills.

  • Introduction to Microsoft Outlook
  • Sending and receiving emails
  • Managing email folders
  • Organizing contacts
  • Understanding calendars and appointments
  • Managing tasks and reminders
  • Office document organization

6. Practical Office Projects

  • Preparing professional business letters
  • Creating reports using Microsoft Word
  • Preparing salary and financial spreadsheets
  • Creating business presentations
  • Developing a simple database system
  • Completing an integrated office productivity project

Practical Training

At IBTECH, we emphasize practical learning. Students will work on real-world office tasks and projects to develop confidence in using Microsoft Office applications.

  • Creating professional business documents
  • Designing spreadsheets for calculations
  • Preparing charts and financial reports
  • Designing business presentations
  • Creating and managing simple databases
  • Completing practical office assignments

Benefits of the Course

  • Develop essential office productivity skills.
  • Improve your employability and career opportunities.
  • Learn to prepare professional documents and reports.
  • Gain practical spreadsheet and data management skills.
  • Improve presentation and communication skills.
  • Become more efficient in office environments.
  • Receive a diploma upon successful completion.

Who Can Enrol?

This course is suitable for students, graduates, job seekers, entrepreneurs, business owners, office assistants, administrators and anyone interested in developing professional computer and office application skills.

No advanced computer knowledge is required. Basic computer knowledge will be helpful, but beginners are welcome.

Certificate

Students who successfully complete the training programme and meet the required practical assessment standards will receive a Diploma in Microsoft Office Suite from IBTECH Computer Training.
